This page checks the county's results feed once a minute and updates itself, so you can leave it open. New numbers will appear here shortly after the county publishes them.

When to expect updates: the Registrar of Voters says the first results will come after 8pm on election night, then every two hours until all polling places have reported. After that, the county plans updates on Tuesdays and Fridays at 4:30pm until certification.

Statewide races and races that cross county lines may show up later. The county holds those until polls have closed in every county.

About this page

Nevada County results ONLY

Every number here counts only votes cast in Nevada County. In statewide and district races, like Congress or state propositions, someone leading here may not be leading overall. Those races are flagged.

This is an independent, unofficial page. I built it because I wanted an easier way to keep track of local races, with Nevada County contests up top and statewide ones below.

Where the numbers come from: every vote count is pulled straight from the Nevada County Elections Office's public results feed and checked once a minute. Nothing is added, estimated, or projected. If a number here ever disagrees with the county, the county is right.

What I add: the layout, the local / regional / statewide sorting, and the pass-line on measures (50%, 55% for school bonds, or two-thirds). "Leading" just means most votes so far. Results stay unofficial until the county certifies them.

Not affiliated with Nevada County or the Elections Office.
